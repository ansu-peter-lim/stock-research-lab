# Knowledge Registry Policy

`knowledge/` contains durable, reviewed findings—not raw observations,
unvalidated ideas, or convenient summaries.

Each entry must state: claim, scope, definitions, data and experiment IDs,
sample design, validation results, limitations, known leakage checks, status,
owner/reviewer, and date. Use statuses `candidate`, `validated`, `rejected`,
or `superseded`.

Promotion requires reproducible evidence across an adequate sample and review
by the research lead/owner gate. A single experiment may generate a candidate
but cannot by itself create durable knowledge. Amendments create a new version
and preserve the prior decision trail.
